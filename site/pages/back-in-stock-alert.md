# Back in stock alert for any product page

Mark the line on a product page that says whether it can be bought, and Zetlyn tells you when it
changes from sold out to available.

## How it works

1. Paste the product page's address below.
2. Click the availability: *Currently unavailable*, *Sold out*, *Out of stock*.
3. Choose **When it says** and enter `In stock`, or **When it no longer says** and enter
   `Sold out`.

Case does not matter. Zetlyn tells you once, when the text appears or goes. Every other change on
the place is still kept in the watch's history.

## Pages that change on their own

Some shops show a different text on every visit, an A/B test or a box that guesses where you live.
Zetlyn reads a changed place a second time before it believes the change, and learns the texts a
place flips between on its own, so those flips are not reported. A new text always is.

Mark a narrow place, the availability line, rather than the whole buy box. The narrower the place,
the fewer surprises.

## Where the message goes

Email, Telegram, Slack, Discord or a signed webhook, as you choose under Notifications.
