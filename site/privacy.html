What Zetlyn does

You name a public web page and, if you like, a place on it. Zetlyn reads that page every hour (with a subscription as often as every minute) from its own servers and emails you when what you marked changes. Your email address is your account; there is no password.

Your account and your watches

What is kept for an account: the email address, when it was made, whether it was confirmed, whether its free trial was used, whether it has a subscription, and the payment provider's reference for the customer and the subscription. For each watch: the address of the page, a description of the place you marked (its position on the page, its tag, its id and classes, and the text beside it), what was read there, the time and outcome of each check for 90 days, and every change with the value before and after for as long as the watch lasts.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR: it is what providing the service you asked for requires.

When you end a watch, it is deleted with its checks and changes. A watch you never confirmed is deleted after two days. An account without a subscription is deleted 30 days after its last watch ended, with everything in it. To delete your account at once, use "Delete account" on your account's page, which also cancels a subscription; or write to privacy@zetlyn.com.

Confirming by email

The first time an address is used, Zetlyn sends it a link and watches nothing until the link is clicked, so that nobody can have mail sent to an address that is not theirs. The links Zetlyn mails, and the sign-in they open, are 256-bit random values; only a SHA-256 hash of each is stored.

Of every mail Zetlyn sends, it notes when, to which address, its subject and whether it went out, so that a mail that did not arrive can be looked into; the text of the mail is not kept. Each note is deleted after 90 days, also when the account was deleted before. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: our interest in mail that arrives.

Signing in with Google or Apple

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, you do so on their site. They tell Zetlyn your email address and that it is verified; Zetlyn keeps the address, as your account, and nothing else of what they send. They learn that you sign in to Zetlyn; their own privacy policies apply to that. With Apple you may hide your address: Zetlyn then gets an address Apple forwards to you, and mails you there. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Cookies

Only what signing in needs (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is asked for: z, your sign-in, for 90 days or until you sign out. It holds a random value and nothing else, is sent only over https, cannot be read by scripts and is not sent when another site links or posts here. While you sign in with Google or Apple, and only then, a second one, zo, holds for ten minutes the random values that tie their answer to your sign-in; it has to come back with Apple's answer, which Apple posts from its own site. There are no cookies for analytics or advertising, and no third-party scripts or fonts on zetlyn.com.

Showing you the page to mark

To let you click the place to watch, Zetlyn reads the page on its servers and shows it to you without its scripts. A page whose content its scripts make is read in a browser that runs on Zetlyn's server (Google Chrome, which sends nothing to Google from there: no crash reports, no usage data). The page's images, styles and fonts are loaded through Zetlyn too, so the site you watch sees Zetlyn's servers, not your browser or your IP address. Every later reading is done by Zetlyn's servers alone.

Hosting

Zetlyn runs on servers rented from Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, in its German data centres. Hetzner acts as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. Hetzner makes a backup of the whole server each day and keeps the last seven, in the same data centres; what is deleted from Zetlyn is gone from the backups after seven days.

Mail

Confirmation, sign-in and change mails are sent through the mail server of netcup GmbH, Daimlerstraße 25, 76185 Karlsruhe, Germany, which acts as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. It sees the address a mail goes to and what it says.

Telegram, Slack, Discord and webhooks

Only if you add one under Account, Notifications: Zetlyn sends each change of your watches (the page's address, what was there before and what is there now, and a link to the watch) to the channel you named, and keeps its address (for Telegram, the number of the chat with Zetlyn's bot) and how the last delivery went. Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC, Dubai), Slack (Slack Technologies, USA) and Discord (Discord Inc., USA) are then told this by your choice, under their own terms and privacy notices; a webhook goes to the address you gave. This is done to fulfil our contract with you, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. Removing the channel, or sending /stop to the bot, ends it and deletes what Zetlyn kept of it.

Paying

A subscription is paid at Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland, which takes the card or the account, the billing address and the VAT number, and sends the invoices. We never see the card. We tell Stripe how many watches beyond those in the plan an account runs, as a number, so that they can be invoiced. From Stripe we keep whether a subscription is active and Stripe's reference for the customer and the subscription. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, and for what tax and commercial law require us to keep, Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR; invoices are kept for the ten years those laws require. Stripe's own notice is at stripe.com/privacy.

Server logs

The web server in front of Zetlyn keeps no access log. The program logs errors, each check (the watch, its outcome and the page's address) and the address each mail is sent to, in the server's system log, which removes the oldest entries as it fills.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we process what you send in order to handle the enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR. We delete it once the matter is settled and no retention obligation stands against it.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You can reach us at privacy@zetlyn.com.
