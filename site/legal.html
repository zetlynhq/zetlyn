What Zetlyn does with other people's pages

Zetlyn reads public web pages that its users name, at most once a minute, from its own servers, and emails those users when what they marked on a page changes. It does not publish the pages it reads or what it finds on them.

If you run a site and want Zetlyn to stop reading it, write to abuse@zetlyn.com. Zetlyn reads with the user agent ZetlynBot and does not try to get around a site that refuses it.
